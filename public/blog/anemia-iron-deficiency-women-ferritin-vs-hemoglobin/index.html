The Hidden Stages of Iron Deficiency:

Iron deficiency develops in three distinct phases:

  1. Stage 1 (Depleted Iron Stores): Ferritin drops below 30 ng/mL. Red blood cell count and Hemoglobin remain technically normal, but patients already suffer fatigue, diffuse hair shedding, and sleep disturbances.
  2. Stage 2 (Iron-Deficient Erythropoiesis): Transferrin saturation plummets, and RBC production begins slowing down.
  3. Stage 3 (Overt Iron-Deficiency Anemia): Hemoglobin drops below clinical thresholds (under 12.0 g/dL in non-pregnant adult women), leading to shortness of breath, palpitations, pale skin, and dizziness.

Why Ferritin Testing Is Indispensable:

If you only test Hemoglobin, you may be told your blood report is "normal" while your body is starving for cellular iron. Checking Serum Ferritin provides an exact metric of your body's iron savings account, enabling targeted dietary adjustments or oral supplementation under medical supervision.
