About the ABO Blood Group & Rh Typing test

Determines ABO blood group and RhD status. Pregnancy, transfusion planning or when blood grouping is required.

Before your sample

No fasting is needed. Weak or discrepant Rh typing needs established laboratory resolution procedures.

Understanding the result

Blood group reports do not substitute for crossmatching before transfusion. Use an authorised transfusion service for urgent transfusion decisions.

Clinical safety: A laboratory test is one part of clinical assessment. Do not delay urgent medical attention for chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or serious illness.
