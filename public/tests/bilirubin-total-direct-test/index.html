About the Bilirubin, Total & Direct test

Measures total and direct bilirubin; indirect may be calculated from the difference. Investigation of jaundice or selected liver/biliary conditions.

Before your sample

Fasting is often not essential, although the lab may advise preparation. Bilirubin by itself cannot determine the cause of jaundice.

Understanding the result

Bilirubin interpretation depends on direct versus indirect predominance. Liver enzymes and clinical evaluation may be needed.

Clinical safety: A laboratory test is one part of clinical assessment. Do not delay urgent medical attention for chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or serious illness.
