Liver & Kidney Function Tests (LFT & KFT): Silent Organ Health Indicators & Normal Ranges

👨‍⚕️ Dr. Ananya Rao, MD (Pathology) 📅 12 November 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read ✓ Verified Clinical Article

📋 LFT & KFT Profiles At a Glance

Sample Type:Clot Activator Serum (Venous Blood)
Fasting Protocol:8-10 Hours Fasting Recommended
Key Liver Analytes:Bilirubin, SGOT (AST), SGPT (ALT), ALP, Total Protein, Albumin
Key Kidney Analytes:Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea, BUN, Uric Acid, Electrolytes
BJSL Tariffs:Creatinine ₹50 | LFT ₹360 | Renal KFT ₹320

The Liver: The Body's Biochemical Powerhouse:

The liver processes nutrients, synthesizes essential blood proteins, metabolizes pharmaceutical drugs, and produces bile for digestion. Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD) has become widespread in urban populations due to refined diets and sedentary routines.

  • SGPT (ALT) & SGOT (AST): Intracellular liver enzymes. When hepatic cells suffer inflammation or injury, these enzymes leak into circulation. Elevated ALT is particularly specific to liver stress.
  • Serum Bilirubin (Total & Direct): Waste product of red blood cell breakdown. Elevated levels cause visible jaundice, dark urine, or scleral yellowing.
  • Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP): Highlights bile duct obstruction, gallstone congestion, or bone turnover.

The Kidneys: Precision Blood Filtration:

Each kidney contains roughly one million microscopic nephrons filtering waste and excess fluid around the clock. Because early renal compromise produces zero outward symptoms, blood biomarkers provide the earliest warning.

  • Serum Creatinine: The gold standard filtration biomarker. Muscle metabolism produces creatinine at a steady rate; elevated levels indicate diminished glomerular filtration.
  • Blood Urea & BUN: Breakdown products of dietary proteins. Rises in tandem with dehydration, high-protein intake, or renal filtration impairment.
  • Uric Acid: Byproduct of purine metabolism. High serum levels can crystallize in joints (gout) or form kidney stones.
