Why Wait for Symptoms When You Can Prevent?

Hypertension, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol, and fatty liver are nicknamed "silent killers" because they develop gradually over years without pain, fever, or physical discomfort. By the time chest heaviness or fatigue appears, systemic vascular damage may already have occurred.

How to Prepare for Your Health Checkup:

  1. 10–12 Hours Fasting: Essential for accurate Fasting Blood Sugar and Lipid Profile (triglycerides). Plain water is permitted and encouraged to keep veins well-hydrated for easy phlebotomy.
  2. Morning Timing: Schedule sample collection between 7:00 AM and 9:30 AM to reflect true circadian fasting baseline.
  3. Medication Routine: Regular blood pressure medications can generally be taken with a sip of water unless specifically advised otherwise by your treating physician.
  4. Avoid Vigorous Exercise: Do not engage in heavy gym workouts or marathon running on the morning of testing, as muscle exertion temporarily elevates SGOT, CPK, and creatinine.
