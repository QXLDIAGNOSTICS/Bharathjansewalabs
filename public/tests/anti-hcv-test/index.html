About the Hepatitis C Antibody (Anti-HCV) test

Detects antibodies to hepatitis C virus. Hepatitis C exposure screening under clinical guidance.

Before your sample

No fasting is needed. HCV RNA testing is usually used to determine current infection after a reactive antibody result.

Understanding the result

A positive antibody result does not by itself establish current active infection. A negative antibody test very early after exposure may need follow-up.

Clinical safety: A laboratory test is one part of clinical assessment. Do not delay urgent medical attention for chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or serious illness.
