Frequently Asked Questions About Our Collection Hubs

What are the standard operating hours across all centres?

All 10 Bharath Jan Sewa Labs centres operate from Monday to Saturday: 07:00 AM to 08:00 PM, and on Sunday: 07:00 AM to 02:00 PM. Please call beforehand to verify specific test cutoff times.

Do I need to book an appointment before visiting a centre?

Walk-ins are always welcome during operating hours. However, messaging us on WhatsApp or calling +91 98055 43143 in advance allows our team to prepare your vacutainers and confirm fasting requirements.

How are samples tested and what are the accreditation standards?

Samples collected at our centres or via home collection are placed in vacuum barcoded vacutainers and transported in temperature-controlled carriers to verified NABL-accredited partner laboratories where applicable, using automated robotic analyzers with strict internal quality controls.

How soon will I receive my test reports?

Routine test reports (such as Complete Blood Count, Blood Glucose, HbA1c, Thyroid TFT, and Urine Routine) are delivered on the same day within 4 to 8 hours directly to your WhatsApp and Email as an encrypted digital PDF.
