About the Dengue NS1 Antigen & IgM/IgG Antibodies (ELISA) test

Tests dengue NS1 antigen and dengue IgM/IgG antibodies as listed in the combination offer. Investigation of suspected dengue based on symptoms and illness day.

Before your sample

No fasting is generally required. Confirm the exact combination and methods before booking.

Understanding the result

Test performance depends on the day of illness; a negative result does not automatically rule out dengue. Warning signs such as bleeding, severe abdominal pain or lethargy need urgent evaluation.

Clinical safety: A laboratory test is one part of clinical assessment. Do not delay urgent medical attention for chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or serious illness.
