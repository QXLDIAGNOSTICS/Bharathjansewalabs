About the Serum Iron test

Measures circulating serum iron concentration. Part of evaluation of suspected abnormal iron status.

Before your sample

Collection time and fasting may matter; check with the laboratory. Ferritin and transferrin saturation provide complementary information.

Understanding the result

Serum iron varies during the day and does not assess iron stores by itself. Avoid interpreting isolated serum iron as a diagnosis of iron deficiency.

Clinical safety: A laboratory test is one part of clinical assessment. Do not delay urgent medical attention for chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or serious illness.
