Deciphering Your Complete Blood Count (CBC) Report: What Each Parameter Tells You

👨‍⚕️ Dr. Ananya Rao, MD (Pathology) 📅 12 November 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read ✓ Verified Clinical Article

The Complete Blood Count (CBC) is the most frequently requested diagnostic blood panel. It examines three major blood cell components produced in bone marrow: Red Blood Cells (RBCs), White Blood Cells (WBCs), and Platelets.

Understanding Key CBC Markers:

  • Hemoglobin (Hb): Oxygen-carrying protein in red blood cells. Low levels indicate anemia.
  • Total & Differential WBC (TLC/DLC): Immune defense cells. Elevated counts signal active viral/bacterial infection or systemic inflammation.
  • Platelets: Cell fragments essential for blood clotting and wound healing.
