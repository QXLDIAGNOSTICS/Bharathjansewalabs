About the Serum Magnesium test

Measures magnesium concentration in blood. Assessment of electrolyte disturbances or selected neuromuscular symptoms.

Before your sample

No fasting is usually required. Haemolysis can interfere with some magnesium measurements.

Understanding the result

Serum magnesium may not perfectly reflect total-body stores. Potassium and calcium may be checked as clinically indicated.

Clinical safety: A laboratory test is one part of clinical assessment. Do not delay urgent medical attention for chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or serious illness.
