About the Complete Haemogram (CBC, ESR & Peripheral Smear) test

Combines CBC, ESR and peripheral-smear examination as advertised. Assessment of anaemia, blood-cell abnormalities or inflammation.

Before your sample

Fasting is usually not needed. Confirm the smear review scope before booking the ₹230 package.

Understanding the result

Peripheral smear findings require clinical interpretation and are not a diagnosis by themselves. CBC and smear results may warrant further directed tests.

Clinical safety: A laboratory test is one part of clinical assessment. Do not delay urgent medical attention for chest pain, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding or serious illness.
