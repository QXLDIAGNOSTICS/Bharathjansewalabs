Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I see approved test prices?

All routine and specialized test prices are published openly on our individual test pages and price list. You can view 66 test prices online without requiring any prior registration or login.

Can I request doorstep home sample collection?

Yes. Doorstep blood collection is available across South, West, Southeast, Central, and North Bengaluru. Call +91 98055 43143 or message us on WhatsApp to check slot timings and nominal collection fees, if applicable.

How are samples processed and what are your accreditation standards?

Samples collected through Bharath Jan Sewa Labs centres and home collection are transported via validated cold-chain carriers and processed through verified NABL-accredited partner laboratories where applicable, using automated diagnostic equipment and daily internal quality controls.

How soon will I get my blood test reports?

Routine test reports (such as Complete Blood Count, Blood Glucose, HbA1c, Thyroid TFT, and Urine Routine) are delivered on the same day within 4 to 8 hours directly to your WhatsApp and Email as a secure PDF.
