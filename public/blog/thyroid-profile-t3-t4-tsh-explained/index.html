Understanding Thyroid Hormones:

The thyroid gland situated in your neck produces T3 (Triiodothyronine) and T4 (Thyroxine) hormones regulated by TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) from the pituitary gland.

  • Hypothyroidism (Underactive): High TSH with low T3/T4 levels, causing weight gain, lethargy, cold intolerance, and dry skin.
  • Hyperthyroidism (Overactive): Low TSH with high T3/T4 levels, causing rapid heartbeat, anxiety, and weight loss.
