How Home Sample Collection Works: Sterile Vacutainer Procedures & Cold-Chain Transport

👨‍⚕️ BJSL Quality Assurance Team 📅 12 November 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read ✓ Verified Clinical Article

Safe, Hygienic Doorstep Phlebotomy:

All BJSL home collection visits utilize sterile, single-use disposable Vacutainer needle systems opened right in front of the patient. Samples are immediately sealed in temperature-controlled cold-chain carrier boxes to maintain specimen integrity en route to NABL-accredited processing labs.
